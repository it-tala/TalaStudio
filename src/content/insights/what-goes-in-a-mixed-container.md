A mixed container carries pieces from more than one collection. It lets a retailer test a range without committing a full container to one item.

## What to check

- **Minimum order.** Each piece has its own minimum quantity.
- **Carton data.** Size, weight and volume decide how much fits.
- **Lead time.** Confirm the schedule for every piece in the load.
- **Inspection.** Ask to see QC photos before loading.

## Plan the load

List the pieces you want, then add up the volume. Our product pages show carton size, CBM and pieces per 40HQ to make this simple.
